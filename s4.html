Overview

New Zealand is a dreamlike destination for Indian travellers - offering the perfect blend of adventure, nature, culture, and comfort. Whether you're planning a romantic honeymoon, a scenic family trip, or a once-in-a-lifetime road journey, this island nation delivers unmatched beauty with warm hospitality.

Indian tourists love how easy and safe it is to explore with wide roads, friendly locals, and well-developed tourism infrastructure. Popular cities like Auckland, Queenstown, Rotorua, and Christchurch are hubs of activity, yet never far from mountains, lakes, and coastlines.

Food is no challenge either there's a growing Indian cuisine presence, plus plenty of vegetarian and vegan-friendly options across the country.

Highlights include the breathtaking fjords of Milford Sound, Māori cultural experiences, natural spas in Rotorua, and skydiving or heli-hiking in the South Island. Self-drive trips are extremely popular and allow travellers to explore at their own pace - whether it's glaciers, glowworm caves, sheep farms, or snow-capped peaks.

New Zealand is especially suited for couples & honeymooners, adventure seekers, nature and wildlife lovers, family holidays and film tourism (Lord of the Rings, Hobbiton fans)

With direct connections via Australia or Southeast Asia and a reputation for being safe, scenic, and peaceful - New Zealand is a spectacular escape waiting to be discovered.
